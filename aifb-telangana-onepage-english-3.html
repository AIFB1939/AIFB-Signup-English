All India Forward Bloc — Youth Wing

StepForward.

Founded by Subhas Chandra Bose in the fight against colonial rule, the Forward Bloc has always been carried by its youngest members. The All India Youth League is where that work continues.

1928
Year the League was founded — one of India’s oldest youth political organisations
01 / Work

Jobs Over Slogans

Pushing for real employment guarantees and an economy that doesn’t force young people to migrate for work.

02 / Equity

Class Struggle

Standing with labour and peasant organisations against concentrations of wealth and power.

03 / Sovereignty

Self-Reliance

Reducing economic and political dependence on external powers.

04 / Voice

Organised Dissent

Training a generation to argue, organise, and hold power accountable.

All India Youth League

The AIYL runs campus units, study circles, and the ground-level work of local campaigns, working alongside the party’s other wings as part of a larger movement.

Organise YourCampus.

Fee hikes, hostel issues, and the fight for education rights start in the classroom. AISB — the Forward Bloc's student wing — organises student voices across colleges and universities.

1939
The party’s founding year — and the tradition AISB carries forward on campuses
01 / Education

Affordable, Quality Education

Pushing back against fee hikes and demanding accessible public education for all.

02 / Housing

Hostel Rights

Standing up for safe, adequate housing and fair mess charges.

03 / Voice

Campus Democracy

Fighting for union elections and student representation that holds administrations accountable.

04 / Future

Job Security

Demanding policy change so a degree actually leads to meaningful employment.

All India Forward Bloc — Labour Wing

Dignity ForLabour.

The Trade Union Coordination Centre (TUCC) — the Forward Bloc’s labour wing — has fought for workers’ rights in factories, among farm labourers, and in the unorganised sector since 1970.

1970
Year TUCC was founded — organising millions of workers nationwide
01 / Wages

Minimum Wage Enforcement

Fighting for a legal minimum wage and timely payment in every sector.

02 / Security

Job Security

Standing against unjust dismissals and contract-labour exploitation.

03 / Welfare

Social Security

Pressing for pensions, insurance, and healthcare to reach unorganised workers too.

04 / Rights

Right to Organise

Protecting the right of workers to form unions and bargain collectively.

Trade Union Coordination Centre

TUCC organises unions across factories, transport, farm labour, and public-sector employment, and negotiates wage settlements.

All India Forward Bloc — Peasant Wing

For The Land.For Justice.

All India Agragami Kisan Sabha — the Forward Bloc’s peasant wing — organises farmers and farm labourers for land rights, fair prices, and debt relief.

1939
The peasant struggle has been central to the party’s mission since its 1939 founding
01 / Land

Land Reform

Fighting for tenant farmers’ land rights and fair land redistribution.

02 / Price

Fair Crop Prices

Demanding a minimum support price and timely procurement for crops.

03 / Debt

Debt Relief

Protecting farmers from predatory moneylenders and expanding institutional credit.

04 / Labour

Farm Labourers’ Rights

Standing with farm labourers for minimum wage and secure working days.
